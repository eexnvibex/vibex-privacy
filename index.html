VIBEX Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

VIBEX (“the app”, “we”) is a party game for friends. This policy explains what information the app uses and what it does not. We keep it simple: we do not ask you to create an account, and we do not sell your data.

1. No account, no email, no password

VIBEX does not require you to sign up or log in. We do not collect your email address, phone number, contacts, or location.

2. Camera and microphone

VIBEX asks for camera and microphone access only so you can record short “dare” clips during the game. Recording happens on your device. Clips are not uploaded to our servers. You decide whether to save a clip to your photo library or share it with other apps. You can deny or change these permissions at any time in your phone settings and still play without recording.

3. Information used in game rooms

If you play on one phone (Solo mode), game information stays on your device. If you create or join a room with friends on different phones, the player names, avatars, scores, selected deck, and room code are sent to our server so everyone in the room can see the same game. Room information is used only to run the game.

4. Data stored on your device

The app saves small settings on your phone, such as your sound on/off choice and your age confirmations. This information stays on your device and is removed if you uninstall the app.

5. Server logs

Like most online services, our hosting provider may automatically record technical information such as IP address and request time for security and to keep the service running. We do not use this to identify you.

6. Analytics, ads and tracking

VIBEX does not currently include advertising, analytics, or tracking tools. If this changes in a future version, we will update this policy before the change.

7. Sharing

We do not sell your information. We do not share it with third parties except our hosting providers, who process it only to run the service, or when required by law.

8. Age and mature content

VIBEX is intended for people aged 13 and older. The app asks you to confirm that you are 13 or older. One optional deck, “Late Night Club”, contains edgier content and is available only after you confirm you are 18 or older. VIBEX is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Your choices

You can deny camera and microphone permissions, uninstall the app to remove all data stored on your device, or contact us to ask about or request deletion of any room information.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version and its date will always be on this page.

11. Contact
